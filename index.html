About

I like problems where fluid dynamics, heat and hardware meet.

I'm finishing an M.Tech in Thermal & Propulsion at the Indian Institute of Space Science and Technology, after a B.Tech in Aerospace Engineering from Amity University. My final-year project is in GTRE's Afterburners and Exhaust Systems Division, running reacting-flow CFD and working on liner cooling and nozzle design.

Outside the lab I build things: RC aircraft, a hovercraft, model rockets, a neural-network surrogate for nozzle design and the trajectory simulator this page borrows its orbit from.
